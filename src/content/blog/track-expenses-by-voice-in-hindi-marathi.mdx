Most expense tracker apps are built for people who enjoy filling out forms. Pick a category, enter an amount, choose a date, add a note. By the time you are done, you have forgotten why you bothered.

AiXpense takes a different approach. You just talk to it — in whatever language feels natural.

## How voice input works in AiXpense

Tap the microphone button in the chat input. Speak your expense. Done.

The app uses **Sarvam AI's saaras:v3** model for speech recognition, which is purpose-built for Indian languages. It understands:

- **Hindi:** "Aaj lunch pe 350 kharch kiya"
- **Marathi:** "Petrol bharla 800 rupaye"
- **Hinglish codemix:** "Uber se office gaya, 220 laga"
- **English:** "Groceries 1500 from DMart"

The spoken text is transcribed and sent to the AI, which extracts the item, amount, date, category, and tags — then saves the expense instantly.

## Why 22+ Indian languages matter

Most voice apps are trained on Western English. Accents, code-mixing, regional phrases — they all trip up generic speech models.

Sarvam's model is specifically trained on Indian speech patterns. It handles:

- Regional accents across states
- Code-mixing (switching between Hindi and English mid-sentence)
- Informal, conversational phrasing ("chai pe gaya, 30 ka")
- All major Indian languages: Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, Assamese, and more

## Voice input on mobile

AiXpense is a web app that works in your mobile browser. Open [aixpense.in](https://aixpense.in), tap the mic, and your phone's microphone activates. No app download needed.

This makes it especially useful when you are on the go — stepping out of a rickshaw, leaving a restaurant, or walking back from a shop. Speak the expense before you forget it.
